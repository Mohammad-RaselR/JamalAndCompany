Design Manifesto

The Principles of Restraint

We reject ephemeral design fads and superficial ornamentation. Our work is anchored in permanent architectural truth: proportion, honesty of materials, and precision detailing.

01 / MATERIAL TRUTH

Honesty of Substance

We do not fake materials. Marble is authentic stone cut from the quarry; timber is kiln-dried solid hardwood; architectural steel is patinated by hand. Authentic textures age with dignity.

Nero Marquina Solid Oak Raw Brass
02 / PROPORTION & VOID

Negative Space as Luxury

True opulence is not clutter; it is the generosity of space and the calmness of an uncluttered visual field. We carve sightlines that promote emotional tranquility and mindfulness.

Golden Ratio Sightline Geometry Minimalist Rythym
03 / BESPOKE JOINERY

In-House Millwork Fabrication

Off-the-shelf cabinetry cannot achieve seamless architectural alignment. We fabricate all bespoke wardrobes, kitchen systems, and credenzas inside our dedicated Dhaka woodworking atelier.

Concealed Hardware Zero Reveal Acoustic Soft-Close
04 / TURNKEY ENGINEERING

Single-Point Accountability

From structural demolition and MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) to smart HVAC distribution and fine art curation, Jamal & Co. executes every phase under single-contract responsibility.

Act XVIII Compliance Structural Safety MEP Integration
05 / LIGHT SCULPTURE

Calibrated 2700K Illumination

We compose light like an architect composes walls. Indirect cove illumination, glare-free architectural lenses, and magnetic track systems highlight textures while keeping sources invisible.

CRI > 97 Concealed Coves DALI Dimming
06 / TROPICAL ACCLIMATIZATION

Engineered for Dhaka Climate

Bangladesh's monsoon humidity and seasonal shifts destroy standard imports. We treat all timber, stone, and metals with proprietary breathable sealants tested for decades of resilience.

Moisture Barrier Anti-Warp Kilning UV Stable Finishes
