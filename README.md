## Overview

**Jamal & Company Limited** is an architectural interior design and turnkey construction practice headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh. The studio specializes in minimalist luxury residences, executive corporate suites, and bespoke spatial architecture across Gulshan, Banani, Baridhara Diplomatic Zone, and broader Dhaka.

This production-ready web application embodies a **monochrome Scandinavian and Swiss modernist architectural design philosophy** inspired by Notion and world-class architectural publications, delivering zero visual noise, authentic materiality, and rich interactive client tools.

---

## Key Features & Highlights

1. **Monochrome Editorial Aesthetics**:
   - Deep obsidian dark mode (`#0c0d0e`) paired with gallery light mode (`#fafaf9`).
   - Clean typographic pairing: *Syne* (modern grotesque display), *Playfair Display* (editorial serif accents), *Plus Jakarta Sans* (clean UI body), and *Space Mono* (architectural technical metadata).
   - Bespoke micro-cursor follower with magnetic hover states.

2. **Official Incorporation Verification**:
   - Features the verified **Certificate of Incorporation** under the Companies Act XVIII of 1994 (`C-172356/2018`, dated `10/10/2018`).
   - Interactive verification modal showcasing the authentic soft copy and Registrar of Joint Stock Companies (RJSC) details.

3. **Selected Works & Interactive Project Drawer**:
   - Filterable portfolio categories: *All Disciplines*, *Residential*, *Commercial & HQ*, *Bespoke Joinery*.
   - Interactive slide-out specification drawer with technical architectural blueprints, material palettes, timelines, and direct inquiry sync.

4. **Before & After Turnkey Transformation Slider**:
   - Interactive touch and mouse drag comparison revealing raw concrete shell handovers transformed into finished architectural sanctuaries.

5. **Notion-Style Design Manifesto**:
   - Modular philosophy blocks exploring *Material Truth*, *Negative Space*, *In-House Joinery*, *Single-Point Turnkey Engineering*, *2700K Calibrated Lighting*, and *Dhaka Climate Acclimatization*.

6. **Tactile Materiality Archive**:
   - Interactive swatch selector (*Nero Marquina Marble*, *Roman Raw Limestone*, *Smoked Belgian Oak*, *Brushed Gunmetal*, *Belgian Flax Linen*).

7. **Interactive Spatial Budget & Scope Calculator**:
   - Dynamic quotation engine estimating total turnkey investment brackets in BDT and execution timelines based on floor area (1,500 – 15,000 sq ft) and custom architectural modules.
   - One-click sync to the commission inquiry form.

8. **Live Dhaka Studio Status & Clock**:
   - Dynamic real-time BST (UTC+6) timekeeper with studio status indicator.
